# Request indexing on Google: your daily list

Google lets you request indexing for **about 10 pages a day**. This list puts the pages people search for most first. Work through one "Day" block each day; it takes about 10 minutes.

**How, for each link:**
1. Open [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) → property **saralkit.com**.
2. Paste the link into the search bar at the top ("Inspect any URL in saralkit.com") and press Enter.
3. If it says **"URL is on Google"**: nothing to do, tick it.
4. If it says **"URL is not on Google"**: click **Request indexing**, wait about a minute until it confirms, then tick it.
5. If you see **"Quota exceeded"**: stop for today and continue tomorrow.

You don't have to request every page. The sitemap already told Google about all 702 pages; requesting just moves the important ones up the queue. Pages not listed here (the other translated pages) will be picked up from the sitemap on their own. After the last day, check **Indexing → Pages** to see how many are indexed.

**Bing:** in [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters) → **URL Submission**, you can paste many links at once (up to the daily limit shown there). Copy whole Day blocks.
